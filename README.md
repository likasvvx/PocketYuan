# 🐉 PocketYuan — Student Budget & Stipend Tracker

**PocketYuan** is a lightweight, mobile-first progressive web application designed specifically for international students studying in China. It helps manage university stipends (**CNY**) alongside financial support from parents (**RUB** / **USD**) without stress.

## ✨ Key Features

- **Multi-Currency Auto-Conversion:** Log income and transfers in **CNY (¥)**, **RUB (₽)**, or **USD ($)** with customizable exchange rates.
- **Smart Category Limits:** Track spending across 5 essential student categories (*Canteen & Food, Clothes & Lifestyle, Metro & Taxi, Dorm & Bills, Study & Books*) with visual progress bars so big one-time purchases stay within planned monthly quotas.
- **Safe Daily Budget:** Automatically calculates how much you can safely spend per day until your next stipend payout.
- **Interactive Animated Mascot:** A custom vector SVG dragon reacts in real time to your financial habits — cheering and jumping when you add funds or optimize spending, and getting sad when monthly limits are exceeded.
- **Offline-First & Private:** Stores all transactions locally on the device (`localStorage`) with full edit and delete support, working seamlessly even without an internet connection.
- **Light & Dark Themes:** Smooth pastel UI with a built-in theme switcher and a one-time onboarding guide.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **HTML5 & Semantic Markup**
- **CSS3** (Custom Properties, Flexbox/Grid, Keyframe Animations, Mobile-First Responsive Design)
- **Vanilla JavaScript (ES6+)** & **Vector SVG Graphics**

## 📱 How to Use on Mobile

1. Open [https://github.io](https://github.io) in Safari (iOS) or Chrome (Android).
2. Tap **Share → Add to Home Screen** (or **⋮ → Add to Home screen**) to install it as a standalone app on your phone.
